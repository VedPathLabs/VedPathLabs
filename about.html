With over 15 years of rich clinical practice, Dr. Dhwani Bhatt brings comprehensive expertise to VED PATHOLOGY LABORATORY. Having completed a prestigious Fellowship in Hemato-oncology at GCRI (The Gujarat Cancer & Research Institute), Dr. Bhatt holds specialized knowledge in complex blood disorders, hematological malignancies, and advanced clinical laboratory diagnostics.

At Ved Pathology Laboratory, every test is conducted under strict quality protocols using computerized analyzer technology. Dr. Bhatt personally reviews and verifies patient reports, ensuring that clinicians and patients receive clear, reliable, and actionable medical results.
