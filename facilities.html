Comprehensive Laboratory Facilities

Providing accurate diagnostic solutions for patients, hospitals, and referring doctors across Ahmedabad.

High Quality Reports Verified by Expert Pathologist

Every report is personally reviewed, authenticated, and signed off by Dr. Dhwani Bhatt M.D. (Pathology), Fellow in Hemato-oncology at GCRI with over 15 years of pathology experience.

Fully Automatic Computerized Laboratory

Equipped with fully automated biochemistry analyzers and high-precision hematology cell counters. Fully computerized workflows guarantee error-free results and fast digital delivery.

Home Visit and Sample Collection Services

Convenient, hygienic home sample collection service. Our trained phlebotomists follow strict sterile protocols to collect blood, urine, or fluid samples at your doorstep.

Testing of Blood, Urine, Stool, Biopsy & Fluid

Complete pathology coverage including detailed hematology blood profiles, urine microalbumin/routine, stool microscopy, tissue biopsy diagnostics, and body fluid examination.

Tailored preventive health profiles designed for early risk detection, organ wellness, chronic disease management, and preoperative screening.
